**目标:**
- 自我介绍并回答租房相关问题（Introduce yourself and answer rental-related questions）

**挑战:**
1. 告诉房东你的租房需求和预算  
    Tell the landlord about your rental needs and budget
2. 回答有关你生活方式和偏好的问题  
    Answer questions about your lifestyle and preferences
3. 讨论你租房的长期计划，以及它们如何与你的工作或生活安排相一致  
    Discuss your long-term rental plans and how they align with your work or living arrangements
4. 向房东询问房屋设施、租期以及周边环境的相关信息  
    Ask the landlord about the house amenities, lease terms, and surrounding area.
